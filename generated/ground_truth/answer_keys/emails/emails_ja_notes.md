## Traps / data-quality issues (what is wrong → correct reading)

1. **OSA01 tax-inclusive sales (山本)**: on 09-01, 09-03, 09-08, 09-11, 09-15 and 09-18 the store manager reported **税込 (VAT-inclusive, ×1.10)** sales from the POS daily sheet and worked out 達成率 against the tax-exclusive budget. That inflates achievement by about 10 pts (e.g. 09-18 reported 8,278,600 / 130.9%, but the true figure is 7,526,000 / 119.0%). Only the 09-08 mail is labelled "（税込）". 鈴木 points this out on 09-18 (`_re1`) and she confirms it on 09-19 (`_re2`). From 09-21 onwards she reports 税抜. Reports by 中村 and 松井 were always net.
2. **TYO01 10-17 tax-inclusive figure (佐藤)**: "売上5,340万" is tax-inclusive. The true net figure is 48,545,800 (163.3%). 田中 corrects it at the top of the 10-18 mail.
3. **TYO02 10-16 typo**: Ito wrote Sales 12,511,100. A correction 15 minutes later (`_corr`) gives 12,151,100, which is the true figure.
4. **TYO02 10-07 mail has subject "2026/10/06(火)"** (copy-paste). The body and numbers belong to **10-07 (Wed)**: 4,471,000.
5. **TYO02 09-21 visitors**: Park writes 入店 157. The KPI figure is 175 (transposed digits).
6. **TYO01 09-25 duplicate**: the same mail was sent twice, 2 minutes apart (`_dup`). Count it once.
7. **Late / missing reports**: TYO01 09-09 is missing. Its sales (812万) appear in the 09-10 mail. The TYO01 10-07 report was sent 10-08 09:47. TYO02 09-14 is missing (figures in the 09-15 mail). TYO02 09-28 was never reported. OSA01 09-24 is missing (figures in the 09-25 mail). OSA01 10-10 appears only in the 10-11 weekend recap.
8. **OSA01 09-26 13:12 "来週シフト"**: a staff shift message sent to the ops list by mistake, with an apology at 13:20. It carries no sales signal, but it does show the store planned full staffing for Golden Week 10-01 → 10-04.
9. **OSA01 09-28 out-of-office**: 鈴木's auto-reply (09-28 → 10-02 business trip). He still answers TYO01 on 09-30 ("limited access").
10. **OSA01 10-06**: the body only says "see attachment". The data is in `OSA01_日報_20261006.xlsx` (net 6,847,500, 119.7%).
11. **Budget % conventions differ**: 田中/高橋/山本 use achievement (sales/budget). Ito rounds it to an integer. The KPI file's `vs_budget_pct` is the variance (achievement − 100).
12. **Mis-typing / 変換ミス**: TYO01 10-20 "異常です" (should be 以上です). OSA01 09-17 "機能に続いて" (should be 昨日に続いて). TYO02 10-20 "waitlsit". These are cosmetic.
13. **Phone demand is not in the POS/unmet data**: the pink IRIDIA phone volumes ("電話10件以上", "30件以上", "20件以上") are staff impressions. They are bigger than the in-store `unmet_requests`. Treat them as real but unquantified demand, not as exact counts.
14. **TYO01 09-13 spike (198% of budget, NOVA black ×18)**: the store says a Chinese family group bought in bulk. Nothing in the scenario explains it, so treat it as a one-off and do not extrapolate.
15. Temperatures quoted by staff (28–33 °C) are what they felt or read. They broadly match a warm spell (27–29 °C max, 09-28 → 10-11), but they are not measurements.
